01 / WEB APPLICATION

Creatr.page

One place for a creator's online world.

A creator-focused link-in-bio platform that brings social channels, priority links, drops, and offers together in customizable, mobile-friendly profiles.

The idea

Give creators a central destination for their content and a way to manage the links their audience sees.

What I built

  • Customizable profiles designed for mobile browsing.
  • Tiered subscription plans and scheduled links.
  • Click analytics to help measure engagement and understand traffic.

02 / PYTHON & AUTOMATION

Flight Deal Finder

Less searching. Better-timed alerts.

A Python application that searches for discounted flights and sends notifications when it finds a deal.

The idea

Automate repeated flight searches and bring useful price updates directly to the user.

What I built

  • Flight search integration through the Tequila Flight Search API.
  • A backend that updates deals as prices change.
  • Automated email and SMS notifications using Twilio, with Sheety integration.
